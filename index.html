Troubleshooting an IP camera connection, in order

Work the checks in order and change one setting at a time: power and link state, local network permission, credentials, stream path, then codec. Preserve the exact error at each step instead of retyping the address and starting over.

  • Inspect the port and service setting in the manufacturer's instructions rather than assuming a shared Wi-Fi name proves access.
  • A camera web page responding does not settle whether its video service is reachable; authenticate with the addressed device's account.
  • If it connects but shows no moving video, separate the address result from the codec result and record both.

The full checklist, kept in this order with a repeatable result, is on IP Camera Hub's camera connection troubleshooting guide. This document structures the record. It does not certify any camera or app.
